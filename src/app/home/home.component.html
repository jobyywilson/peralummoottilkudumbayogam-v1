A shared heritage

One family,
many generations.

Peralummoottil Kudumbayogam brings together the descendants of Thommy of the Karavadesseril branch of Pakalomattom Tharavadu.

Arriving from Kuravilangad around 1700, Thommy settled at Vellikkara in Kallooppara. From those roots, our family has flourished across Central Travancore and far beyond—united by memory, kinship and a commitment to future generations.
