Our gathering storyReorganised in 1973. Carried forward by every Sakha.

The first Annual General Body meeting after the reorganisation of the Kudumbayogam was held at the residence of Shri. K.C. Issac, Kottackal Puthuparampil, on Thursday, 27 December 1973, under the committee formed in April that year.

Based on an entry in the 1917 diary of Kottackal GeeVarghese Chandy, the 1973 gathering is estimated to have been the 57th annual meeting. Since 1975, a different Sakha has hosted the gathering each year under the guidance of the Managing Committee.
